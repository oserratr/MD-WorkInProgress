## What I want to explore with this project ?

So I made a list of several interactions that are recurrent in the context of the transmission of cultural heritage.

![Schema](/IMAGES/formes-transmission.jpg)

> First visual diagram of the transmission spectrum

- So I wanted to explore oral and visual transmission for the first prototype

![Schema prototype test days](/IMAGES/ideas-test-days-2.jpg)

> I tested this prototype with several players, at least 2 each time. The first player listens to a story told orally without the other hearing it. In front of him are different assets as well as a sheet where as he listens he can remove or add visual elements. At the end, he ends up transmitting what he understood of the story with his visual support.

_demo test_

**With this prototype, what I wanted to know ?**

- See how players visually re-appropriate a story told,
- See if oral transmission combined with visual transmission improves the understanding of the story to other players
- See the liberties that players can take with the story (add, remove, exaggerate parts or details of the story)

_end demo test_

**What did I learn ?**

- audio too fast not allowing to have the necessary understanding or the time to place the desired elements, time constraint that could have distorted the tests?
- testing with more abstract forms would allow to give more freedom to the players on his interpretation of the story and leaves him control on how he wishes to transmit this story to another player
- too many visual choices
- mechanics of being able to both remove and add elements is a good thing, allows the user to "appropriate" the story to help him correctly transmit the key elements
- in the transmission, the players are content to be "synthetic" when they tell the story that they have previously listened to, even if depending on the person certain elements are "exaggerated" whether in the choice of visuals (size, recurrence) as well as in the oral narration
