### Update of story

**Synopsis** : After his grandmother’s death, the main character helps his mother sort through her belongings. He discovers a mysterious object along with a letter. But he quickly faces a problem: the letter is written in his mother’s native language — one he never really learned, as it was only spoken within the close maternal family. When he tries to ask about it, no one wants to talk. It’s as if the object and letter are tied to a secret or a taboo.

**Goal** : Translate a letter and understand the purpose of the mysterious object (to be specified)
