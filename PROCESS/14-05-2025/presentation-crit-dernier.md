After his grandmother's death, you must help the main character's mother sort through her belongings. He discovers a mysterious object accompanied by a letter. But she quickly runs into a problem: the letter is written in his mother's native language, a language he never really learned, as it was only spoken within his immediate maternal family. When she tries to question him about it, no one wants to talk about it. It's as if the object and the letter are linked to a secret, a taboo.

This game is a 3D point-and-click game in which you play as a member of a family living in a country other than their country of origin.

It's a detective game in which you must use subterfuge to translate a mysterious letter and try to understand the family's history, impacted for several generations by the traces of colonization.

IN THE GAME

→ You start in your grandmother's attic. You can interact with a few items to understand the cultural mix.
→ Then you can interact with your mother, who seems to be reading something.
→ So, she asks us to bring down the last box.
→ After putting the box down, you dropped something, a mysterious object.
→ Your mother asks you to be more careful and what object fell.
→ Then, you can inspect the object.

AFTER

→ The idea is that afterward, you can discuss it with your mother, who seems upset at the sight of this object. She also informs us that there is a letter, but that we shouldn't pay attention to this object and put it away because our cousin is coming to help remove all the boxes.
→ And the idea is that, using the shape of the object, you must find the letter in the room.
→ Once found, you will have to work with your cousin to translate the letter throughout the game, with the threat of the mother taking the letter back.
→ The cousin helps "validate" the translated words, because he was born and raised in the country of origin.
